# GranMonte Thai Wine — US Market Launch Playbook
### Pre-Sell First, Stock Later Strategy
**Prepared for:** Up (Khetpapol Limphoka) | **Date:** March 30, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

GranMonte is Thailand's most awarded winery with **zero US distribution**. There are ~5,500 Thai restaurants in the US spending $800–$2,500/month on wine — yet none can offer Thai wine to their customers. This is a white-space opportunity with a built-in story.

**The play:** Pre-sell to Thai restaurants in Florida first (Up's home state), collect soft commitments, then place a minimum order with GranMonte. Zero inventory risk until demand is proven.

## 3. Licensing & Legal Structure (Florida)

### Licenses Needed

| License | Cost | What It Does |
|---------|------|-------------|
| **TTB Importer's Basic Permit** (federal) | Free to apply | Allows importing alcohol into the US |
| **COLA** (Certificate of Label Approval) | Per product | Required for each wine label before import |
| **FDA Food Facility Registration** | Free | Required for all food/beverage importers |
| **Florida IMPR License** | $500/year | Import license — buy from GranMonte, sell to distributors |
| **Florida JDBW License** | $25,000 surety bond | Distributor license — sell directly to restaurants in FL |
| **PASS Registration** | $15/year per brand | Primary American Source of Supply for GranMonte |

### The Dual-License Play (IMPR + JDBW)

By holding both an IMPR (importer) and JDBW (distributor) license in Florida, Up can act as **both importer AND distributor** — cutting out the middleman and keeping distributor margins (~28-33%) in-house.

> ⚠️ **Get an alcohol beverage attorney to confirm this structure before proceeding.** Florida tied-house laws prohibit affiliation with retail venues. Budget ~$2,000-3,000 for legal setup.

## 4. The Pre-Sell Strategy — Step by Step

### Phase 1: Build the Target List (Week 1-2)

**Goal:** Identify 50-100 qualifying Thai restaurants in Florida.

**Qualification Criteria:**
- Mid-range to upscale (avg check $25+/person)
- Already carries wine on menu (not beer-only)
- Independently owned (not chain/franchise)
- Active Google/Yelp presence with 4+ stars
- Located in metro areas: Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, Gainesville

**How to build the list:**
1. Google Maps API / Yelp Fusion API — search "Thai restaurant" by city
2. Filter by rating, price range, review count
3. Check each restaurant's website/menu for wine list
4. Capture: name, address, phone, owner name (if findable), current wine list, Instagram/Facebook

### Phase 2: Craft the Pitch (Week 2)

**The pitch is NOT "buy our wine." The pitch is "be the first Thai restaurant in Florida to serve Thai wine."**

**Pitch Materials Needed:**
1. **One-pager** (PDF) — GranMonte story, awards, 3-4 wines with tasting notes + food pairings
2. **Price sheet** — Wholesale cost per case, suggested menu pricing, margin analysis showing restaurant makes MORE per bottle than generic wines
3. **Sample request form** — "We'd love to bring you a tasting. When works?"
4. **Soft commitment form** — Simple: "I'm interested in carrying [X] cases/month of GranMonte wines when available. Non-binding."

### Phase 3: Outreach Campaign (Week 3-6)

**Outreach Sequence (per restaurant):**

| Step | Timing | Channel | Action |
|------|--------|---------|--------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Instagram DM | Follow + genuine comment on their food post. No pitch yet. |
| 2 | Day 2 | Email/DM | Intro: "Hi [name], I'm bringing Thailand's most awarded wine to the US for the first time. Your restaurant caught my eye because [specific reason]. Would love to chat for 5 min." |
| 3 | Day 5 | Phone call | Follow up. Ask about their current wine list, what pairs well with their food. Listen more than talk. |
| 4 | Day 7 | In-person visit | Bring the one-pager. Ideally bring a sample bottle (personal, not for sale). Eat at their restaurant. |
| 5 | Day 10 | Follow-up | Send the price sheet + soft commitment form. "No pressure — just gauging interest before we place our first import order." |
| 6 | Day 14 | Close | Call to collect the soft commitment or understand objections. |

**Language:** Use Thai with Thai owners. This is community selling, not cold B2B.

### Phase 4: Validate & Order (Week 7-8)

**Minimum viable validation:**
- **20 soft commitments** at 2+ cases/month each = 40+ cases/month demand
- That's ~480 cases/year = ~5,760 bottles = ~5% of GranMonte's production
- Enough to justify a first container order

**Once validated:**
1. Contact GranMonte with proof of demand (soft commitment count + restaurant list)
2. Negotiate FOB pricing, MOQ, payment terms
3. Place first order
4. Complete federal + state licensing (if not already done)

## 5. Pricing Model

### Working backwards from restaurant menu price:

```
Target menu price:           $42/bottle
÷ Restaurant markup (3x):   $14 (restaurant's cost per bottle)
÷ Distributor markup (1.0x): $14 (Up IS the distributor — dual license)
- Up's margin (25%):         $10.50 (Up's landed cost target)
- Shipping/duties/tax (~40%): $7.50 (FOB target to GranMonte)
```

### If Up holds the JDBW distributor license, the math gets much better:

| Scenario | Without Distributor License | With Dual License (IMPR+JDBW) |
|----------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| GranMonte FOB | $8 | $8 |
| Landed cost (US warehouse) | $11 | $11 |
| Up's sell price to distributor | $14 | — |
| Distributor sell to restaurant | $19 | $15 (Up sells direct) |
| Restaurant menu price (3x) | $57 | $45 |
| **Up's margin per bottle** | **$3** | **$4** |
| **Restaurant gets lower cost** | No | **Yes — more competitive menu price** |

**Win-win:** Dual license means Up makes more AND restaurants pay less. This IS the pitch.

## 8. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| GranMonte FOB too high for US margins | Medium | Negotiate volume pricing, start with entry-level wines (Spring, Heritage) |
| Soft commitments don't convert to orders | Medium | Get specific: "2 cases of Chenin Blanc, 1 case Rosé per month" not just "I'm interested" |
| Licensing takes too long | Low | Start TTB application NOW — it can take 60-90 days |
| Thai restaurant owners aren't interested | Low | The story + community angle is strong. If FL doesn't bite, pivot to LA (largest Thai community in US) |
| GranMonte already finds another US importer | Low | Move fast. Being first matters. Exclusive US distribution agreement is the goal. |
| Cash flow crunch between order and revenue | Medium | Pre-sell covers this. Also: negotiate 30-60 day payment terms with GranMonte |
