Perfect Pairings

Designed for the dishes you already serve

Each wine was crafted in Thailand, by a Thai winemaker, for Thai cuisine. These aren't suggestions — they're the pairings Nikki herself recommends.

Green Curry
→ Spring Chenin Blanc
The honeydew sweetness of the Chenin tames coconut cream richness while its acidity lifts the kaffir lime and Thai basil aromatics.
Pad Thai
→ Sakuna Rosé
Bright acidity meets tamarind sweetness. The strawberry notes complement the peanut garnish beautifully.
Massaman Curry
→ Heritage Syrah
Dark fruit and warm spice in the Syrah mirror the cardamom, cinnamon, and star anise in this rich, slow-cooked curry.
Tom Yum Goong
→ Sakuna Rosé
The rosé's citrus profile echoes lemongrass and galangal while its light body doesn't overpower the delicate shrimp.
Grilled Meats & Crying Tiger
→ The Orient Syrah
A flagship wine for flagship dishes. The smoky oak and dark fruit intensity matches chargrilled beef with nam jim jaew perfectly.
Mango Sticky Rice
→ Spring Chenin Blanc
Off-dry tropical Chenin with ripe mango and sweet coconut cream? It's like they were meant for each other — because they were.
