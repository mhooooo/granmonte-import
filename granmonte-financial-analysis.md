## 1. SKU Portfolio & Margin Analysis

### Per-Bottle Economics

| SKU | Wholesale | Restaurant Price | Margin/Bottle | Markup on Cost | Category |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|---------------|----------------|----------|
| The Orient Syrah | $28 | $70 | **$42** | 150% | Red Premium |
| Heritage Syrah | $22 | $55 | **$33** | 150% | Red |
| Spring Chenin Blanc | $18 | $45 | **$27** | 150% | White |
| Sakuna Rosé | $16 | $40 | **$24** | 150% | Rosé |

**Key insight:** All SKUs carry identical 60% restaurant margin and 150% markup on cost. The Orient Syrah generates the highest absolute margin at $42/bottle but requires more sophisticated positioning. Sakuna Rosé at $40 is the easiest entry point for Thai restaurants currently offering beer/spirits only.

### Recommended SKU Mix Strategy (per restaurant/month)
- **Starter accounts**: 60% Rosé/White, 40% Heritage Syrah (avg ~30 bottles)
- **Mature accounts**: 30% Rosé/White, 40% Heritage, 30% Orient (avg ~45 bottles)
- **Blended average wholesale per bottle**: ~$21 (weighted across SKU mix)
