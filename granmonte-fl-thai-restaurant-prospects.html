Executive Summary

This prospect list identifies 55 qualifying Thai restaurants across Florida for GranMonte wine distribution outreach. Restaurants are prioritized into three tiers based on wine program sophistication, price point, seating capacity, reputation, and owner accessibility.

Regional Breakdown

  • Miami / Fort Lauderdale / South Florida: 25 restaurants -- Deepest market with highest concentration of upscale Thai dining. Several have extensive wine lists. Start here for Up's home-base advantage.
  • Orlando / Central Florida: 8 restaurants -- Growing market with new upscale entries (TALAY downtown). Tourism traffic drives volume.
  • Tampa / St. Petersburg: 9 restaurants -- Strong market with Thai Prime (200 seats, full bar) as anchor prospect.
  • Jacksonville: 5 restaurants -- Smaller market but several quality establishments with wine service.
  • Southwest FL (Naples/Sarasota/Fort Myers): 6 restaurants -- Affluent clientele, wine-friendly market.
  • Other FL Cities: 2 restaurants -- Gainesville/Tallahassee secondary markets.

Key Qualification Notes

  • Wine list confirmed: 18+ restaurants with verified wine programs (Thai Spice, Sky Thai Sushi, Atchana's, Drunken Poet, TALAY Orlando, Lemongrass Thai Bistro Jax, etc.)
  • Michelin/Awards: Lung Yai (Bib Gourmand), Larb Thai-Isan (Michelin Guide), Lemon Grass Tampa (Best Thai 6x), Thai Spice (Zagat Top 10)
  • Multi-location chains: Moon Thai (8+ locations), Komoon (3 locations), Lemon Grass Tampa (2 locations) -- single deal = multiple placements
  • GranMonte story angle: "First Thai winery" narrative resonates uniquely with Thai restaurant owners -- cultural pride + differentiation on wine list

Recommended Outreach Strategy

Phase 1: South Florida Blitz (Week 1-2)

  • Visit in person: Thai Spice, Sky Thai Sushi, Atchana's, BKK Thai, Bangkok Bistro -- these are the Tier 1 prospects with confirmed wine programs closest to Up's base
  • Bring tasting samples: 2-3 bottles of GranMonte (Syrah Rosé, Chenin Blanc, and a red) for the owner/sommelier to try
  • Story pitch: "GranMonte is the only premium winery in Thailand, from Khao Yai. Your Thai restaurant is the natural home for Thai wine. No other restaurant in Florida carries it yet -- you'd be first."
  • Moon Thai corporate: Contact Jack Punma (founder) -- one deal = 8+ placements

Phase 2: Central FL + Tampa (Week 3-4)

  • Orlando: TALAY (downtown, speakeasy), Lim Ros (Winter Park), Coco Thai (I-Drive volume)
  • Tampa: Thai Prime Kitchen (200 seats!), Lemon Grass (6x award winner), Siam Garden (35-year institution)
  • Bundle approach: Offer introductory case pricing with consignment option for first order

Phase 3: Jacksonville + Southwest FL (Week 5-6)

  • Jacksonville: Lemongrass Thai Bistro (established wine program), Senyai (new + trendy)
  • Southwest FL: Drunken Poet (boutique wine focus), Star Thai Siesta Key (affluent beach), Komoon (3 locations)

Key Selling Points for GranMonte

  • "Thailand's premier winery" -- cultural pride for Thai restaurant owners
  • "Only Thai wine available in Florida" -- first-mover advantage, exclusivity
  • Perfect food pairing story: wine literally made to complement Thai flavors
  • Customer talking point: diners love the novelty of "Thai wine with Thai food"
  • Higher margin potential: unique product = less price sensitivity
  • By-the-glass opportunity: $12-15/glass at $40-50/bottle retail, strong margins
