The GranMonte Story

In 1999, Visooth and Sakuna Lohitnavy planted the first vines on a 12-hectare plot in Khao Yai's Asoke Valley -- and proved the world wrong about Thai wine.

Nestled at 350 meters elevation in the foothills of Khao Yai National Park, GranMonte is Thailand's most acclaimed winery. The volcanic red clay soil, cool mountain breezes, and dramatic day-to-night temperature swings create a terroir unlike anywhere else in the world -- producing wines that have stunned critics from Vienna to London to Tokyo.

Today, their daughter Nikki Lohitnavy -- Thailand's first and only fully qualified oenologist, trained at the University of Adelaide -- crafts 23 distinct wines across 100,000+ bottles annually. GranMonte exports to seven countries including France and Switzerland. Their wines have earned gold medals at the Decanter World Wine Awards, AWC Vienna, and the distinction of "Best Wine from Asia" at the Hong Kong International Wine & Spirit Competition.

This is not a novelty. This is a world-class winery that happens to be Thai.
