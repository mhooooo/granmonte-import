Bottom Line for Up

This is a low-risk, moderate-reward play. Total startup cash needed is under $15K (initial inventory + licenses + marketing). Even worst-case conservative scenario generates $17K profit Year 1. The real upside is building the distribution infrastructure — once 20 accounts are proven, scaling to other states is incremental. The FL JDBW license is the only gating item — resolve it or use LibDib to start selling immediately.
